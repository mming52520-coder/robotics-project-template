# Code of Conduct

We welcome respectful, evidence-based collaboration across disciplines and experience levels. Be constructive, explain technical disagreement, protect private information, and make safety concerns easy to raise.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, unsafe pressure to operate hardware, and knowingly submitting proprietary or restricted material are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Report concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
