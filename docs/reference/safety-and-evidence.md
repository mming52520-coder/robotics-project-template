# Safety and evidence / 安全与证据

Simulation, fake transport, and offline replay are the default. A DesignPackage must define bounded motion, one safety gate, stop conditions, command expiry, watchdog behavior, fault recovery, and a human physical-trial review.

默认采用仿真、虚拟传输和离线回放。DesignPackage 必须定义有界运动、唯一安全门、停止条件、命令过期、看门狗、故障恢复，以及人工实机试验评审。

A successful unit, integration, simulation, or replay check is evidence for one stated condition. It is not a safety certificate and never authorizes physical actuation.

单元、集成、仿真或回放检查通过，只能证明相应的明确条件；它不是安全认证，也绝不授权实机运动。
